How to play

A few quick turns can turn a mystery into a word.

1

Read the puzzle

Look at the word with missing letters, or read the clue for the answer.

2

Spin the reels

Swipe each letter reel up or down until the highlighted letters spell your guess.

3

Press CHECK

Correct letters lock in gold. Adjust the rest and try again to complete the word.
